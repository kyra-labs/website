The studio

Software meant to be used, not just installed.

Kyra Labs is an independent app studio — not a registered company, but a personal brand under which apps are designed, built, shipped, and supported end to end by a single developer.

The focus stays narrow on purpose: everyday tools that solve one real problem well, without the ads, dark patterns, or bloat that follow most “productivity” apps.
