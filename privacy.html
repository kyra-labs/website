Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Kyra Labs (referred to as "we", "us", the "studio") operates kyralabs.dev. This policy covers this website only. Spenzia and any other Kyra Labs app has its own privacy policy, linked from that app's page and its Play Store listing.

What we collect

This site collects the minimum needed to respond to you:

  • Email address — only if you submit it through the waitlist or contact form, so we can notify you about app launches or reply to your message.
  • Basic server logs — standard hosting logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability. We do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To send you a one-time notification when a new app or feature launches, if you joined a waitlist.
  • To reply to messages sent through the contact form or email.

We do not sell, rent, or share your email with advertisers or third parties. We do not use it for anything beyond what you signed up for.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails are kept until the relevant app launches (at which point we send the one notification and you can ask us to delete it) or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Email kyra.labs.3011@gmail.com at any time to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted. We'll action it within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. Your browser may store basic technical preferences (like theme or scroll position) locally on your device; these never leave your browser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Kyra Labs is an independent studio run by one developer, not a registered company. For anything privacy-related, reach out at kyra.labs.3011@gmail.com.
